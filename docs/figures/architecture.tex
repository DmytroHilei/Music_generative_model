vel. & velocity: how hard the note is played (32 levels) & LaBSE \rcite{1} & multilingual sentence encoder (109 languages)\\
compound token & one sequence position = one whole note & CFG \rcite{8} & classifier-free guidance: push logits away from the caption-free prediction\\
caption vector & a caption-adapter output placed in the sequence; no target of its own, but the last vector of a section caption predicts the next note & DRY \rcite{9} & ``don't repeat yourself'' sampler: penalises continuing a passage that copies an earlier one\\
null vectors & learned stand-ins for ``no caption'' & BOS / EOS & start / end of piece: pitch values 128 / 129\\
teacher forcing & training feeds the true earlier values, not samples & KV cache & keys and values of earlier positions kept between steps (18 heads per layer)\\
multi-hot & a 0/1 vector with a 1 for every instrument present & level & a bin of a continuous input with its own learned vector\\
